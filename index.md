# Filterwise

Filterwise is a free Chrome extension (early beta) that hides YouTube videos matching your own
keyword, channel and Shorts rules. Filtering runs on your device: no account, no analytics, no
servers.

- [Privacy policy](privacy.md)
- [Support and problem reports](support.md)

Filterwise is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by
YouTube or Google.
